WHAT'S RUNNING UNDERNEATH

Not just a pretty front end

01
FAQ chatbot with lead capture

A patient-facing chat widget answers common questions and, on a miss, collects name, phone and reason — or hands off to the clinic's WhatsApp with the question pre-filled.

02
Multi-user admin logins

Real accounts with per-section permissions replace a single shared password, so different staff can safely manage only the content sections they own.

03
Trilingual by design

Every page — not just the menu — is authored in English, Bahasa Malaysia and Chinese, switchable instantly without a page reload.

04
One-tap appointment booking

Appointment and enquiry buttons open WhatsApp directly to the clinic's number with a pre-written message, meeting patients where they already are.

05
Installable, offline-friendly

As a PWA on a custom domain with SSL, the app can be added to a phone's home screen, run fullscreen on a clinic tablet, or installed as a standalone Windows kiosk app on a dedicated front-desk PC.

06
Live in production

Runs on a custom domain (drchandranir.info) with SSL, auto-deploying from Git through an on-demand serverless hosting platform — every update goes live without manual server work.
