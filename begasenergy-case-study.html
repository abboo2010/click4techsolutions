Begas Energy: A Corporate Site Backed by Its Own Server

A public-facing website for an oil and gas maintenance contractor in Kota Kinabalu, hosted on its own dedicated VPS rather than shared hosting, so the company's business email stays fast, reliable and fully under its own control.

Live at begasenergy.comPrepared by Ravi

The brief

Begas Energy Sdn Bhd is a Sabah-based Bumiputera engineering and construction company, providing Civil, Mechanical and Electrical maintenance services to Oil and Gas majors across upstream and downstream operations. A company operating in that industry is judged in part by how it presents itself to the operators and majors it tenders to — the brief was a site that reads as established and credible, backed by infrastructure the company controls outright rather than a shared hosting account.

Two deliverables in one project

01 · THE SITE

A corporate presence

Company profile, leadership, the full range of services, and a team page introducing the people behind the work — in English, with a built-in translator for other languages.

02 · THE INFRASTRUCTURE

Its own dedicated VPS

Not shared hosting — a server the company doesn't share with anyone else, so business email keeps working reliably whatever else is happening on a shared host.

What the site covers

Company profile. Begas Energy's background, incorporation and leadership are set out on the home page, alongside the full range of services the company offers to Oil and Gas operators: HVAC operation and maintenance, ducting fabrication and cleaning, valve maintenance, electrical switchboard fabrication and commissioning, PLC troubleshooting, civil and structural repair, VSAT data transmission maintenance, pipeline rehabilitation, and general building maintenance and manpower supply.

A named team. A dedicated Our Team page introduces the company by name and role — from the Managing Director and Operation Manager through to Contract, Procurement, Accounts and QA/QC staff — each with their qualifications and years in the oil and gas industry, giving a prospective client real people to recognise rather than an anonymous company name.

Social links built into the header. Facebook, X (Twitter), Instagram, YouTube and WhatsApp are linked directly from every page's header, alongside a one-click email link and a language selector, so a visitor can move straight from the site to the company's other channels or into a WhatsApp chat.

Business email that keeps working. Because the site runs on its own VPS rather than a shared hosting account, the company's business email is not competing for resources with other websites on the same server — it stays fast and reliable, and is fully under the company's own control rather than a shared provider's.

A real production deployment. The site is live on the company's own domain (begasenergy.com), with a full company address, telephone and fax numbers, and a general enquiry email in the footer.

Why a dedicated VPS

Shared hosting puts a company's website and email on the same server as many other, unrelated sites — if one of them is misconfigured, gets flagged as a spam source, or simply drives heavy traffic, every site on that server can be affected, business email included. A dedicated VPS gives Begas Energy a server that answers to no one else's traffic or mistakes, which matters most for the one thing a company can least afford to have go down: the email address its clients and tenders reach it on.
