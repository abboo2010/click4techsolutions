WHAT'S RUNNING UNDERNEATH

Not just a pretty front end

01
Its own dedicated VPS

Not a shared hosting account — a server Begas Energy doesn't share with any other site, so nothing else's traffic or mistakes can affect it.

02
Business email that just works

Because email runs on that same dedicated server, it stays fast and reliable and is fully under the company's own control.

03
A named team

An Our Team page introduces the company's leadership and staff by name, role and experience — not an anonymous company name.

04
The full service range

HVAC, ducting, valves, electrical panels, PLC systems, civil and structural repair, VSAT maintenance and manpower supply, all set out on the site.

05
Social & WhatsApp built in

Facebook, X, Instagram, YouTube and WhatsApp are linked directly from the header of every page.

06
Live in production

Running on the company's own domain, begasenergy.com, with the full company address, phone, fax and enquiry email in the footer.
