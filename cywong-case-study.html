USED COOKING OIL RECYCLING

CY Wong Green Energy: A Recycling Business Built for Convenience

A multi-language website for a Kota Kinabalu used-cooking-oil (UCO) recycling company, with a built-in online form so households and businesses can schedule a pickup without a phone call — backed by an actively managed Facebook page.

Live at cywonggreenenergy.comPrepared by Ravi

The brief

CY Wong Green Energy Sdn Bhd collects used cooking oil from hotels, restaurants and households around Kota Kinabalu and channels it to a third-party biodiesel processor, turning what would otherwise go down a drain or into a landfill into a renewable fuel feedstock. A recycling business like this lives or dies on how easy it is for a household or a restaurant kitchen to actually request a pickup — so the brief centred on a genuine online booking flow, not just an informational page with a phone number.

Two deliverables in one project

01 · THE SITE

An online collection request

A real booking form — property type, preferred date/time, address and postcode — so a pickup can be scheduled directly from the site, in four languages.

02 · THE FACEBOOK PAGE

An actively managed page

4.8K followers and regular local-language posts, giving the business a second, conversational channel alongside the website — the one social platform Click 4 Tech manages for them.

What the site covers

The company's story. An About Us page sets out CY Wong Green Energy's founding in Kota Kinabalu, Sabah, and its core business: sourcing used cooking oil from hotels, restaurants and households and channelling it to a trusted third-party biodiesel processor.

A dedicated education page. A Used Cooking Oil (UCO) page explains what happens to the oil after collection — biodiesel production, industrial uses such as soaps and lubricants, animal-feed additives and composting — giving visitors a reason to recycle beyond "someone will pick it up."

A real booking form, not just a phone number. The Request Collection page is a working form: first/last name, email, a Malaysia-formatted phone field, a property-type selector (commercial building, private property, condominium, apartment), preferred date/time, and full address with postcode — submitted directly, no call required.

Four languages. A language selector in the header switches the whole site between English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Bahasa Melayu, matching the mix of households and businesses the company collects from.

Direct contact built into every page. Facebook, a WhatsApp click-to-chat link and a one-click email address sit in the header of every page, alongside a sticky "Collection Request" WhatsApp banner that follows the visitor down the page.

Why an online booking form matters

Asking someone to phone in to schedule a pickup is a small thing, but it's friction — and friction is what keeps a bin of used oil sitting in a kitchen instead of being collected. Letting a customer pick a property type, a preferred date and time, and their address straight from the site turns a "we should really call them" into a form filled in on the spot, in whichever of the four languages the visitor is comfortable in.

The Facebook page

Of CY Wong Green Energy's social channels, Facebook is the one Click 4 Tech Solutions manages directly: CY WONG Recycle Trading has grown to 4.8K followers, with regular posts in Malay and English covering collection campaigns and community events across Sabah and Sarawak — a second, more conversational front door alongside the website's booking form.
