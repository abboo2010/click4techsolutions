CLINIC WEBSITE & CONTENT

Dharma Ortho: A Clinic Site Built Around Real Cases, Not Stock Photos

A navy-and-gold website for Dr. Dharmalingam Muthiah, Consultant Orthopaedic & Trauma Surgeon at Gleneagles Hospital Kota Kinabalu — with a genuine video case-study library pulled from his own YouTube channel, backed by a managed Facebook, Instagram and YouTube presence.

Live at dharmaortho.myPrepared by Ravi

The brief

Dr. Dharmalingam Muthiah is a Consultant Orthopaedic & Trauma Surgeon offering a wide range of joint replacement, trauma and arthroscopic services. A specialist practice like this earns trust through evidence, not adjectives — so the brief was a site built to actually show real cases and technology (like the VELYS™ robotic knee system) rather than lean on generic clinic stock photography, and content channels the practice could keep adding to on its own.

Two deliverables in one project

01 · THE SITE

18 pages, one clear story

Home, About Dr, a 13-page Services hub, a Gallery with real case photos and videos, and Contact — each service explained on its own page rather than one long list.

02 · THE CONTENT CHANNELS

Facebook, Instagram & YouTube

Three social channels Click 4 Tech manages alongside the site, feeding real case videos and updates back into the Gallery page.

What the site covers

A focused services hub. Thirteen individual service pages — from Orthopaedic Trauma Surgery and Joint Replacement to the VELYS™ Robotic-Assisted Knee Replacement and Arthroscopic (Keyhole) Sports Surgery — each explained on its own page rather than folded into one long list.

A real photo and video gallery. The Gallery page's Photo tab holds real clinical case photography organised by case, and its Video tab embeds real case-highlight videos straight from Dr. Dharmalingam's own YouTube channel — titled cases like a knee injury from netball, avascular necrosis of the hip, and Charcot neuro-osteoarthropathy, with more added as the channel grows.

Click-to-chat built in. A floating WhatsApp button and a "Book Consultation" button follow the visitor on every page, alongside the clinic's WhatsApp number, clinic line and email in the header.

A real production deployment. The site is live on the practice's own domain (dharmaortho.my), with the full Gleneagles Kota Kinabalu clinic address, phone numbers and email in the footer.

The content channels

Alongside the website, Click 4 Tech Solutions manages Dr. Dharmalingam's Dharma Orthopaedics Facebook Page, his @orthodm Instagram account, and his @DHARMA240 YouTube channel — which has grown to 116 videos, the same library the site's Video Gallery draws its case highlights from. Together they give the practice a presence across the platforms patients actually use to look up a surgeon before an appointment.
